Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

BakBak is a free Indian radio app for iPhone. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

What we collect

We collect anonymous usage data to understand how the app is used and to improve it. This includes:

  • An anonymous device identifier — a randomly generated ID stored on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, or any personal account.
  • In-app events such as which stations you play, features you use, and screens you visit.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifiable information.

How we use it

  • To understand which features and stations are most used
  • To identify and fix bugs
  • To improve the overall app experience

We do not use this data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Third-party analytics

Analytics data is processed by Mixpanel. For details on how Mixpanel handles data, see their privacy policy.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained for as long as necessary to improve the app. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Children

BakBak is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
